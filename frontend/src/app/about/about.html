About StudentConnect

One workspace for every part of running a school

StudentConnect brings student records, academic structure, attendance, assessments, homework, learning materials, activities, announcements, reporting, and operational oversight into a single role-aware product — built for administrators, teachers, students, and families.

What we bring together

A single source of truth for the whole school

Every workflow shares the same student, class, and academic-year records — nothing is duplicated or out of sync.

Isolated, multi-tenant workspaces

Each school owns a fully isolated set of users, records, and files — with server-side checks on every request.

Role-aware by design

Admins, teachers, students, parents, and activity coordinators each see exactly what's relevant to them.

Connected communication

Announcements, notifications, and email digests keep families and staff aligned without extra tools.

Reporting built in

Report cards, transcripts, at-risk analytics, and audit history are generated from the same live data.

Why it's built this way

Clear, secure, and dependable operations

Security by default — every record, file, and action is checked against the caller's role, school, and ownership before it's returned or applied.

No hidden data loss — corrections are tracked, drafts stay editable, and published records require an explicit reopen before they can change.

Built for real school workflows — from term locking and rollover to waitlists and grading bands, the details that make school operations work.
